Skills extracted from postings that were never used to build the graph (up to 60 per occupation). Target: the posting's occupation.

HGT is best on simulated profiles, which are drawn from the same graph it trains on. On real postings Node2Vec is slightly ahead, and both graph models clearly beat skill overlap and random ranking. The held-out postings are the more realistic estimate.

Edge-weighting ablation

The same models and test sets with five ways of weighting occupation→skill edges. Real held-out postings.

The proposed weighting (full) improves Node2Vec by about 15% and skill overlap by about 54% over an unweighted graph; confidence and demand each help. Removing posting-freshness damping makes no measurable difference, because the postings span only about two weeks.
